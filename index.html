About Me

I'm a UX/UI designer and marketing strategist focused on creating thoughtful, intuitive digital experiences.

In every project, my process is guided by a few core questions that keep my work intentional and user-centered: What are we trying to solve with this? Why does this need to exist? Who are we designing this for? What should the experience feel like for them? These questions help me move from concept to structure with clarity and purpose.

I'm drawn to design that balances visual clarity with intention — work that feels refined, natural, and effortless to use. I see visual and digital experiences as something that shapes how we move through the world, often without us consciously noticing. My inspiration comes from everyday environments, especially cafés, travel, and thoughtful design details. Outside of work, I recharge through café hopping and exploring matcha spots, which helps me stay grounded and observant.

Outside of design, I'm interested in visual culture, branding in daily life, and emerging creative tools. I also enjoy vibe coding and use AI as a collaborative tool to support ideation, streamline workflows, and expand my approach to problem-solving and design thinking.
